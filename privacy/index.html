BBL TECHNOLOGY LTD ("we", "us", "our") operates the mobile application LoveSync: Couple Quiz (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. We collect the following:

Category What it is Why we collect it
Display name The name you type when you first open the App, used to identify you to your partner inside a quiz room Core app functionality — so your partner sees who they're playing with
Game session data Your quiz answers, room code, and score for the duration of one quiz game Core app functionality — syncing the game live between your phone and your partner's
App activity & crash/diagnostic data (via Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics) Screens viewed, features used, app crashes and performance diagnostics Understanding how the App is used and fixing bugs

We do not collect your real name (unless you choose to use it as your display name), email address, phone number, physical address, photos, contacts, precise or approximate location, financial information, or health information.

2. Anonymous Technical Identity

The App uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to generate a random technical identifier for your device. This happens silently in the background purely so our database security rules can tell "your" game room data apart from someone else's — there is no sign-up, no password, and no link to your real identity.

3. How Game Rooms Work

When you create or join a quiz room, a 6-digit room code links two phones (a Host and a Player) together for the duration of one quiz game. Your display name and quiz answers are synced through Firebase Realtime Database only between those two phones. A room code is used once and is not reused for a new game.

4. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Advertising ID may be shared with our attribution partners (Adjust, Meta, TikTok) solely to measure how the App was discovered/installed. This version of the App shows no advertisements.
  • Crash and diagnostic data is shared with Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics) to help us keep the App stable.
  • We do not share your display name or quiz answers with any third party outside of the two phones in your own game room.

5. Data Storage & Security

All data in transit between the App and our servers (Firebase) is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Your game history (completed quiz results) is stored locally on your device. A finished game's session data on our server is only needed to let both phones display the same result; it is not used for any purpose beyond that game.

6. Your Choices & Data Deletion

  • You can change your display name at any time in Settings.
  • You can delete your locally stored game history at any time via Settings → Delete History.
  • Because the App does not use accounts, there is no account to delete. If you would like any residual session data associated with your device removed from our servers, contact us at contact@bblvn.store and we will action the request without unreasonable delay.

7. Children's Privacy

LoveSync: Couple Quiz is designed for couples (ages 18 and older) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.
